Tidemark

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version: Tidemark doesn’t collect, send or sell any of your data. There is no account and no server. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone unless you choose to export it.

Who runs Tidemark

Tidemark is made by YSB Ventures Ltd (registered in England and Wales, company number 16022339), which is responsible for this policy. Because Tidemark keeps your data on your phone, we never hold it: the only personal information we see is an email you choose to send us.

What Tidemark stores

Your plan, weigh-ins, notes and tags you add to a day, habit ticks, measurements, calorie and protein numbers, session weights, progress photos and, if you use it, the medication you track, the doses you mark as taken, injection sites and side effects you note. If you stop tracking medication, Tidemark asks whether to keep or delete your dose history. All of it is stored only on your device, inside Tidemark’s private storage. Progress photos are kept inside the app and are never added to your photo library.

What Tidemark sends

Nothing. Tidemark has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It doesn’t need an internet connection to work.

Permissions

  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional) locks the app. The check is done by iOS; Tidemark never sees your face or fingerprint data.
  • Camera and photos (optional) are used only when you add a progress photo. The photo is copied into Tidemark’s private storage.
  • Apple Health (optional, off until you switch it on) reads your body weight from Health and saves the weights you log to it, so your scale and other health apps stay in step. Health data is read and written on your iPhone only; Tidemark never sends it to a server, never uses it for advertising, and never stores it in iCloud: automatic backups leave out readings that came from Health. A backup you export yourself includes them, because it's your copy to keep wherever you choose.
  • Notifications (optional) are used only for the daily weigh-in reminder and, if you turn it on, a dose-day reminder. Both are scheduled on your phone.

Backups and exports

When you tap Export, Tidemark creates a file and opens the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, email) is your choice, and it is then handled by that service. Photos are not included in backups.

If you turn on Automatic backup (Settings › Automatic backup), you choose a folder once, on your iPhone or in iCloud Drive, and Tidemark saves a backup file into it on each day your data changes, keeping the newest 14. These files leave out readings that came from Apple Health; Health keeps them, and restoring reads them from Health again. Tidemark looks at the file names in that folder only to tidy its own “Tidemark backup” files, and never opens anything else there. These files aren’t password-protected. If the folder is in iCloud Drive, Apple stores them in your iCloud account like any other file of yours; Tidemark itself never connects to iCloud or any server. Turning automatic backup off, or deleting all your data, stops it; files already in the folder stay until you delete them.

The report for your doctor (a PDF) works the same way: it's made on your phone and goes only where you share it.

How your data is protected

  • Tidemark’s storage uses iOS’s strongest file protection: while your iPhone is locked, its data is encrypted and can’t be read, even by Tidemark.
  • With the optional lock on, Tidemark asks for Face ID (or your passcode) every time you come back to it.
  • The app switcher shows a blank Tidemark screen, never your numbers.
  • Reminders never show a weight, a medication name or any other detail on your lock screen, just a nudge.
  • Backups can be locked with a password (XChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption, key derived with scrypt). Without the password the file can't be read, by anyone, including us. A backup saved without a password, and the CSV spreadsheet, are plain text, so keep those somewhere private. Export files are deleted from Tidemark as soon as the share sheet closes.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS includes Tidemark’s data in that backup, as it does for every app. This is controlled by your iPhone settings, not by Tidemark.

Deleting your data

Tidemark has no account, so there is no account to delete: all your data is on your phone. Settings → Delete all my data removes all of it, photos included, immediately and permanently. Deleting the app also deletes it. Nothing is held anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Your rights

Weight, medication and body measurements are health data, which UK data protection law treats with extra care. That's the reason Tidemark keeps them only on your phone, where you control them. If you email us, we use your email only to reply, because we have a legitimate interest in helping you, and we delete it once your question is dealt with. You can ask us what we hold about you (normally nothing beyond that email), or ask us to delete it, at the address below. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Tidemark isn’t directed at children under 13.

The website and the waitlist

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers. If you join the waitlist, your email address is used for one thing: telling you when Tidemark launches, with the launch price. It's kept by YSB Ventures Ltd (and, once it's set up, the email service that sends that message), never shared or sold, never connected to the app, and deleted after launch. Ask us to remove it at any time by emailing the support address below, or use the unsubscribe link in the email.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Tidemark Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; Tidemark never sees your card, name or Apple ID, only whether this Apple ID has Plus, which it checks with the App Store on your phone and remembers there. There's still no account and nothing of yours leaves your phone.

If Tidemark ever hits an error, it can open an email to support with the error message and app version. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email, and it never contains your weights or other entries. Tidemark has no crash-reporting or analytics service.

Questions, support or a privacy request: email yameen@ysbdesigns.uk. The terms and conditions cover the website, the waitlist and Tidemark Plus.

Not medical advice

Tidemark’s targets, trends and calorie estimates are general guidance worked out from the numbers you enter. They aren’t medical advice; talk to a GP or dietitian before big changes, especially if you’re pregnant or have a health condition.
