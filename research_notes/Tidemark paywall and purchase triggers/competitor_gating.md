# Competitor monetisation and gating for weight-trend and GLP-1 tracker iPhone apps

Observation notes: App Store listings were fetched on 23 Nov 2026, mainly from the GB storefront (apps.apple.com/gb), with the Shotsy US storefront for comparison. App Store in-app purchase (IAP) lists show prices without product names or periods unless the listing says otherwise. Where a price can't be matched to a plan, the note says so. Rating counts are per storefront: GB counts are much lower than US counts.

## 1. Prices, plan types, trials, free vs paid, and paywall timing for each competitor

### Takeaway
Dedicated GLP-1 trackers (Shotsy, Glapp, MeAgain) charge about £30–£40 a year, or £7–£20 a month in the UK. They keep dose logging free and charge for analytics: the medication-level chart, reports, predictions and AI tools. Plain weight-trend apps (Happy Scale, Libra) charge much less, at £11.49–£17.99 a year or a £39.99 lifetime purchase. Calorie and macro apps (MyFitnessPal, Lose It!, MacroFactor) charge £40–£63 a year, and MacroFactor has a hard paywall. Paywall timing is poorly documented. The only direct evidence is a MeAgain review complaining about paying "before you even see the app" (an onboarding paywall), and a Shotsy US review that describes paying after a one-week trial.

### Inferences
- In the UK, a dedicated GLP-1 tracker costs about £30–£40 a year (Shotsy £29.99/£39.99, Glapp £39.99) and £7–£13 a month. Tidemark's £19.99 a year and £3.99 a month sit well below the GLP-1 apps, and above the weight-trend apps (Happy Scale £11.49, Libra £17.99).
- Tidemark's £49.99 lifetime price sits between Happy Scale (£39.99) and Lose It! (£59.99). None of the dedicated GLP-1 trackers I checked shows a lifetime option, so a lifetime purchase could stand out in that segment.
- Shotsy's spread of yearly prices ($39.99/$49.99/$59.99) and monthly prices ($9.99/$14.99) suggests it runs price tests or offers intro and win-back pricing. This is inferred from the IAP list, not confirmed.

### Gaps
- I could not observe paywall screens or timing (onboarding vs feature tap) directly for any app. That needs an install, or Mobbin or Adapty screenshots.
- Trial lengths: Happy Scale (unspecified), MFP, Lose It! and MacroFactor list trials without stating the length. For Shotsy, the 7-day trial comes from one US review.
- I did not fetch the GB listings for Pep and Withings/Renpho. No data was found for Juniper's or Simple Online Pharmacy's apps.
- "Gala" did not come up as an identifiable GLP-1 app.
- Libra's GB IAPs (£1.99/£17.99) conflict with its description, which says €0.99 a month.

## 2. What users praise and complain about (paywalls, pricing, privacy, accounts), plus ratings, downloads and revenue

### Takeaway
The clearest complaints come when features that used to be free move behind a paywall without grandfathering (Glapp), when an app demands payment before the user has seen it (MeAgain), and when data export and deletion are weak. Users praise privacy that needs no tracking (Shotsy) and say they'll happily pay a small sum compared with what the medication costs. Shotsy leads on ratings by a wide margin.

### Cited Findings
- Glapp, 1-star review (18 Jun): the reviewer objects to the £9.99/month price and to weekly reports moving behind the paywall without existing users being grandfathered, and says they'll quit. Another reviewer (20 Jun) complains that data is locked behind the subscription, that there's no proper export, and that it's unclear whether account deletion removes their data. The developer replied that previously generated reports stay accessible. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/glapp-smart-glp-1-tracker/id6756984097)
- MeAgain: one reviewer says the app "makes you pay before you even see the app" and that "most features are not available yet". The developer offered a refund and a free year. — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/meagain-glp-1-all-in-one-app/id6744178534)
- Shotsy US reviews: an annual subscriber says the benefits are worth it, another calls the cost small next to the medication cost, and another praises the absence of tracking or data harvesting. — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shotsy-glp-1-tracker/id6499510249)

- In this category the privacy-first, no-account approach shows up in the most-praised apps: Shotsy and Happy Scale declare only data not linked to you. Tidemark's no-account stance fits the apps users praise, and it can say so in marketing.

## 3. Which features command payment, and where Tidemark is under-priced, over-priced, or missing a paid feature

### Takeaway
Across the GLP-1 apps, people pay for the estimated medication-level chart, reports (doctor PDFs and progress reports), predictions and milestones, AI food scanning and chat, and supply or multi-compound tracking. Tidemark's Plus features fit these patterns, especially dose-linked trends, side-effect patterns and the doctor PDF. However, Tidemark appears to lack an estimated medication-level curve, which every GLP-1 competitor offers. Its monthly price is roughly half what GLP-1 competitors charge.

### Cited Findings
- Medication-level chart: paid in Shotsy (Shotsy+) and Pep (Pro), free in Glapp, and offered by Lose It! (paid status unclear). GlucoPal charges for its level forecast. — [pepglp1.com](https://pepglp1.com/best-glp-1-tracker-apps/); [Lose It! GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/lose-it-calorie-counter/id297368629)
- Doctor and progress reports: Glapp gives 3 free doctor PDFs and charges for unlimited ones; its Progress Reports and Predictions are paid (Max). MeAgain added doctor reports in Nov 2026. GlucoPal Pro includes a PDF report. — [Glapp GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/glapp-smart-glp-1-tracker/id6756984097); [MeAgain GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/meagain-glp-1-all-in-one-app/id6744178534); [pepglp1.com](https://pepglp1.com/best-glp-1-tracker-apps/)
- Apple Health sync is reported as paid in Shotsy. Tidemark gives it free. — [pepglp1.com](https://pepglp1.com/best-glp-1-tracker-apps/)
- Progress photos and widgets are paid in MeAgain (per the blog). Shotsy's Progress widget is free. — [pepglp1.com](https://pepglp1.com/best-glp-1-tracker-apps/); [Shotsy US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shotsy-glp-1-tracker/id6499510249)
- Supply tracking and more than one compound are paid in Regimen. Glapp tracks supply. — [pepglp1.com](https://pepglp1.com/best-glp-1-tracker-apps/); [Glapp US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glapp-smart-glp-1-tracker/id6756984097)
- Food logging (barcode, photo and AI) is paid in MyFitnessPal, Lose It! and Pep Pro. — listings above
- Weight-trend predictions are paid in Happy Scale (per a 2020 review) and in Glapp Max. — [Happy Scale GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/happy-scale/id532430574); [Glapp GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/glapp-smart-glp-1-tracker/id6756984097)
- Some apps use weekly plans: Glapp sells Max at £4.99 a week. — [Glapp GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/glapp-smart-glp-1-tracker/id6756984097)

### Inferences
- **Under-priced:**
  - Tidemark's £3.99 a month sits well below Shotsy (£7.09–£19.99) and Glapp (£9.99). There is likely room at £5.99–£6.99 a month while keeping the annual plan the clear best deal.
  - Tidemark's £19.99 a year sits below Shotsy's lowest yearly price (£29.99). £24.99–£29.99 would still undercut Glapp (£39.99) and match Shotsy's lower SKU. This is judgement, not tested data.
- **Lifetime price:** £49.99 is reasonable next to Happy Scale (£39.99) and Lose It! (£59.99). Because GLP-1 apps don't sell lifetime plans, it may be a selling point.
- **Generous free tier:** Tidemark's free tier gives away Apple Health sync, widgets and the full medication log, all of which some competitors charge for. That is good for reviews and goodwill, but it leaves fewer upgrade prompts in daily use.
- **Possibly missing paid feature:** an estimated medication-level curve (pharmacokinetics). Every major GLP-1 competitor has one, and it is the most common paid hook. Its absence could be a deliberate choice, since the curve is an estimate with medical caveats, but it is the biggest gap against competitors. Supply and refill tracking is another common feature.
- **Doctor PDF:** Glapp's model (3 free reports, then paid) is a proven purchase trigger. Tidemark could give one sample report free, then require Plus.
- **Paywall timing:** a hard paywall at onboarding drew complaints (MeAgain). Shotsy's free core plus paid extras, with a 7-day trial, draws praise. This supports Tidemark's freemium model with prompts when a feature is tapped.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Tidemark's "trend at each dose" maps onto a medication-level curve. I compared it from the brief only.
- I found no conversion or retention benchmarks for GLP-1 tracker paywalls.
- Withings and Renpho companion apps were not researched. Both are generally hardware-led, but this is unverified here.
