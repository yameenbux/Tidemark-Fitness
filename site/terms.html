Not medical advice

Tidemark shows trends, targets, estimates and records worked out from the numbers you enter. They are general information, not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment plan, and they can be wrong if the numbers going in are. The medication log is a record for you and your prescriber; it never tells you what dose to take.

Talk to your GP, prescriber or a dietitian before making big changes, especially if you are pregnant, under 18, taking medication or have a health condition. If you have, or have had, an eating disorder, please speak to your doctor before tracking your weight. Tidemark isn’t made for children under 13.
